# Yuggenda API - Business Rules

## 1. General Rules

* Every resource must belong to a valid business context where applicable.
* Users must be authenticated to manage protected business resources.
* Only business members with appropriate permissions can access or modify business resources.
* Customers do not need an account to create an appointment.
* Registered customers may have their customer record linked to a user account.
* Guest customer information must be sufficient to complete a booking.

## 2. Business Rules

* A business must have a valid name.
* A user must be authenticated to create or modify a business.
* Every business must have at least one `OWNER`.
* The last `OWNER` of a business cannot be removed or have their owner role revoked.
* Business resources can only be accessed by authorized members of that business.

## 3. Business Member Rules

* A business member must belong to an existing business.
* A user cannot have duplicate membership in the same business.
* A `STAFF` member can provide services and have availability.
* Only authorized roles can add, remove, or modify business members.
* Business membership roles must correspond to defined roles.

## 4. Service Rules

* A service must belong to an existing business.
* A service must have a valid name and duration.
* Service duration must be greater than zero.
* A service can only be assigned to staff members belonging to the same business.
* Inactive services cannot be used to create new appointments.

## 5. Customer Rules

* A customer must belong to an existing business.
* A customer may optionally be linked to a registered user.
* Guest customers can create appointments without an account.
* Customer contact information must be valid when required for booking.
* A customer's data must only be accessible within the appropriate business context.

## 6. Availability Rules

* Availability must belong to a valid staff member.
* Availability can only be configured for staff belonging to the same business.
* Availability periods must contain valid time ranges.
* Availability periods must not contain invalid or contradictory time ranges.
* Appointment creation must respect the staff member's availability.
* A staff member may have multiple availability periods on the same day.
* Multiple availability periods may be used to represent breaks within the working day.
* Availability periods for the same staff member and date must not overlap.
* A service appointment must fit entirely within a single availability period.
* An appointment cannot span a gap between availability periods.

## 7. Appointment Rules

### General Rules

* An appointment must belong to an existing business, customer, service, and staff member.
* The customer, service, and staff member must belong to the same business.
* The selected staff member must be assigned to the selected service.
* The appointment duration must match the service duration.
* The appointment must fall within the staff member's available time.
* A staff member cannot have conflicting active appointments.
* Only valid appointment statuses can be used.

### Appointment Statuses

Appointments can have the following statuses:

* `Scheduled`: The appointment has been created and the time slot has been reserved.
* `Confirmed`: The appointment has been explicitly confirmed.
* `Completed`: The appointment has been completed successfully.
* `Cancelled`: The appointment has been cancelled and is no longer an active booking.

New appointments must start with the `Scheduled` status.

### Valid State Transitions

The following state transitions are allowed:

```text
Scheduled
    ├──> Confirmed
    └──> Cancelled

Confirmed
    ├──> Completed
    └──> Cancelled

Completed
    └──> No further transitions

Cancelled
    └──> No further transitions
```

* An appointment cannot transition directly from `Scheduled` to `Completed`.
* An appointment cannot transition from `Completed` or `Cancelled` back to an active status.
* Invalid state transitions must be rejected.

### Cancellation Rules

* `Scheduled` appointments can be cancelled.
* `Confirmed` appointments can be cancelled.
* `Completed` appointments cannot be cancelled.
* `Cancelled` appointments cannot be cancelled again.
* A cancelled appointment is not considered an active booking.

### Rescheduling Rules

* `Scheduled` appointments can be rescheduled.
* `Confirmed` appointments can be rescheduled.
* `Completed` appointments cannot be rescheduled.
* `Cancelled` appointments cannot be rescheduled.
* Rescheduling must validate the new date and time against staff availability.
* Rescheduling must validate that the staff member has no conflicting active appointment at the new time.
* Rescheduling must preserve the appointment's business, customer, service, and assigned staff relationships unless explicitly changed by an allowed operation.


## 8. Authorization Rules

* `OWNER`: full control over the business and its resources.
* `ADMIN`: manages business resources and members according to defined permissions.
* `STAFF`: manages assigned services, availability, and relevant appointments.
* Customers can only access their own permitted customer and appointment data.
* Guest customers do not receive authenticated access to the business management system.


## 9. Timezone Rules

* Each business must define a valid IANA timezone identifier.
* The business timezone is the source of truth for scheduling operations.
* Staff availability is interpreted using the business timezone.
* Available slots are calculated using the business timezone.
* Appointment times are interpreted according to the business timezone when creating or modifying bookings.
* Appointment timestamps represent absolute points in time and should be persisted in UTC.
* The API server's local timezone must not affect business scheduling.
* Client timezone may be used for presentation purposes but must not change the business scheduling rules.


## 10. Date and Time Precision Rules

* Scheduling operations use minute-level precision.
* Appointment start and end times must be aligned to whole minutes.
* Seconds are not meaningful for appointment scheduling and should be stored as zero.
* Service duration is expressed in whole minutes.
* Availability times use minute-level precision.
* Slot generation operates using minute-level precision.


## 11. Slot Generation Rules

* Available appointment slots are generated using a 15-minute base interval.
* Slot start times must be aligned to the 15-minute interval.
* The slot interval is independent of the service duration.
* A slot is considered available only when the complete service duration fits within the staff member's availability.
* A slot is not available when the resulting appointment would conflict with an existing active appointment.
* The slot interval is global for the MVP and is not configurable per business.
